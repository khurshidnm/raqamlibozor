The parking module is one of the most used sections for market administrations. That is why we rebuilt the reports section: entry-exit records, payments and revenue are now brought together on a single screen.

## What has changed

- **Daily, weekly and monthly revenue** — pick a period and see the result right away.
- **Shift comparison** — revenue for each inspector's shift is shown separately.
- **Export to Excel** — a table ready for accounting.
- **List of unpaid exits** — which vehicle left without paying is stored together with the camera record.

## Who benefits

The reports are designed for market management and parking inspectors. Managers follow the overall figures, while inspectors follow their own shift. All data is updated in real time, so there is no need for manual calculations at the end of the day.

The new reports are enabled automatically for all customers. If you have questions, contact us by requesting a demo.
