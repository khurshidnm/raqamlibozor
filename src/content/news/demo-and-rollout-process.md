Many market managers ask the same question: how long does it take to introduce the system, and will it disrupt trading? The answer: usually a few weeks, and trading does not stop.

## 1. We identify your needs

We visit your market and get to know the stall layout, the parking entry-exit points and the existing cameras. On that basis we decide together which modules you need.

## 2. We demonstrate the system

During the demo, stall occupancy, payment control and rent records are shown with samples that resemble real data. The compatibility of existing cameras is also checked at this stage.

## 3. We plan the rollout

The required equipment, scope of work, timeline and price are agreed. Short training is held for staff, and then the system is launched step by step.
